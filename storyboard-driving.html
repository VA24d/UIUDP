AeroDrive Simulations

Interactive Scenarios for Safety, Performance, and Discovery

1

Autonomous Cruising

The baseline state: 65 km/h on highway. The HUD confirms "AUTONOMOUS MODE" with a blue accent, ensuring Alex feels the system's stability.

"System active. Enjoy your journey."
2

Construction Takeover

An unmapped construction zone is detected. An 8-second countdown initiates with amber strobe effects and a HUD takeover alert.

"Take manual control: Construction Zone."
3

Fatigue Recovery

Sensors detect driver drowsiness. The car escalates from soft chimes to a jarring buzzer, engaging emergency autonomous override.

"SOS: Fatigue detected. Calling services."
4

Predictive Range

Algorithms predict range will fall short of the next charger. A reroute modal appears, offering a diversion to a high-speed station.

"Insufficient range detected. Reroute to charger?"
5

Journey Guide

Alex activates Touring Mode. The car provides contextual commentary on landmarks, turning a routine drive into an exploration.

"On your left: The historic Old Town Hall."
6

Safe Return

After any scenario, the system resets to its trusted autonomous state. The HUD returns to blue, signaling all systems are normal.

"Simulation complete. Resetting to Auto."
