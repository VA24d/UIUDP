# Use cases

## 1- Onboarding

* **Experience Tuning:** Calibrating the vehicle's autonomous behavior (speed, braking, g-forces) to match the individual driver's comfort level.  
* **Driver Identification & Customization:** Automatically recognizes the driver, loading their specific system preferences and profiles instantly.  
* **Interactive Tutorial:** Gamifying and shortening the tutorial and making it more immersive, so users can able to focus and understand the tutorial quickly.


## 2- Driving

* **State-Aware Routing:** Navigating based on autonomous compatibility and suggesting context-aware pitstops like charging or food.  
* **Takeover Requests & Capabilities:** Managing the safe, clear, and staggered transfer of vehicle control between the system and the human driver.  
* **Reading User Behaviour and Pattern:** According to the selected user’s profile, AI can recommend the types of daily routes according to the time when the driver chooses to drive, and also auto-re-route the journey, which can depend on the traffic and the user’s mood.  
* **Auto Suggestion:** Whenever a user visits a new place or a new place opens along their daily route, AI will notify them of the changing environment. If the car is running low on fuel, AI will suggest the most efficient route to refuel.  
* **Smart Alert:** Smart, strong haptic feedback to the user when the car detects the driver’s sleepiness or the sleeping state to alert the rider, and if the same thing happens multiple times, it will also alert their family or emergency contacts of loved ones.

## 3-  Riding

* **Environmental Feedback:** Transparently communicating exactly what the vehicle's sensors perceive in the surrounding environment.  
* **Maneuver Warnings:** Providing advance notice to the driver just before the vehicle executes sudden braking or sharp directional changes.  
* **Productive Time Utilization:** Clearly signaling when the environment is predictable enough for the driver to safely disengage and focus on other tasks.
